# Security Policy

## Introduction

The security of the smart contract is our top priority. We are committed to ensuring the safety of our users' funds and the integrity of our protocol. This document outlines our security policy, how to report vulnerabilities, and the measures we take to keep our smart contract secure.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it to us as soon as possible. We encourage responsible disclosure and will work with you to ensure that the issue is addressed in a timely manner.

Please email me at **<thris.dev@gmail.com>**.

When reporting a vulnerability, please include the following information:

- A detailed description of the vulnerability
- Steps to reproduce the vulnerability
- Any potential impact of the vulnerability

We will acknowledge your report within 24 hours and provide you with a timeline for addressing the issue. We may also offer a bounty for critical vulnerabilities.

## Security Audits

The Lumenn smart contract has not yet been audited by a third-party security firm. We are actively seeking a reputable firm to conduct a comprehensive security audit. Once the audit is complete, we will make the results publicly available.
